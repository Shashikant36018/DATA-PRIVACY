# Practical 2: Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of LeetCode

## Aim

To perform a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of LeetCode by identifying the personal data involved, understanding how it is processed, identifying privacy risks, and suggesting suitable privacy controls.

## Organization

**LeetCode** is an online programming and educational platform where users create accounts, solve programming problems, participate in contests, and interact through user-generated content.

## Personal Data Involved

The assessment considers the following types of data:

- Name and email address
- Account and profile information
- IP address
- Cookies and usage information
- Technical and log information
- User-generated content such as solutions and discussions

## Purpose of Data Processing

The collected information may be used for:

- Providing and maintaining the platform
- Managing user accounts
- Improving website functionality
- Analytics and understanding user activity
- Security and fraud prevention
- Supporting other operational requirements

## Data Processing Flow

```text
User
  ↓
LeetCode Website / Application
  ↓
Data Collection
  ↓
Processing & Storage
  ↓
Service Providers / Analytics
  ↓
Service Delivery
```
| Privacy Risk                | Possible Impact                                    | Risk Level |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Unauthorized access         | Personal or account information could be exposed   | High       |
| Excessive data collection   | Unnecessary information may affect user privacy    | Medium     |
| Tracking and cookies        | User activity may be monitored                     | Medium     |
| Third-party processing      | Information may be accessible to service providers | Medium     |
| International data transfer | Data may be processed in another country           | Medium     |
| User-generated content      | Public content may reveal information about users  | Medium     |
## Privacy Controls

The following controls can help reduce privacy risks:

Data Minimization – Collect only the information necessary for the service.
Encryption – Protect data during transmission and storage.
Access Control – Allow only authorized users and systems to access sensitive information.
User Control – Provide options for account management and data deletion.
Transparency – Clearly explain data collection and processing practices.
Third-Party Controls – Ensure service providers follow appropriate privacy requirements.
Security Monitoring – Regularly identify and respond to security threats.

## Recommendations
Regularly review the data being collected and remove unnecessary information.
Clearly explain the use of cookies and tracking technologies.
Give users better visibility and control over their personal information.
Periodically assess third-party data processors.
Clearly communicate data retention and deletion practices.
Conduct privacy assessments whenever major system or policy changes are introduced.
Conclusion

The PIA identifies several privacy risks related to data collection, tracking, third-party processing, international transfers, and user-generated content. Appropriate controls such as encryption, access control, data minimization, transparency, and regular privacy reviews can help reduce these risks.

Overall Privacy Impact: Medium
