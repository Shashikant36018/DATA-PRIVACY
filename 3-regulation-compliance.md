# Practical 3: Regulation Compliance Assessment of LeetCode

## Aim

To examine the privacy regulations applicable to LeetCode and assess how its privacy practices address important legal and regulatory requirements related to personal data protection.

## Organization

**LeetCode** is an online programming and educational platform that collects and processes user information while providing coding problems, contests, discussions, and other services.

## Applicable Privacy Regulations

Some important privacy regulations that may be relevant to an online platform such as LeetCode include:

- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)** – Applies to the processing of personal data of individuals in the European Economic Area under applicable circumstances.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act)** – Provides privacy rights to eligible California residents.
- **Other Applicable Laws** – Additional privacy and data protection requirements may apply depending on the user's location and the nature of data processing.

## Compliance Assessment

| Regulation / Requirement | Relevant Privacy Area | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR | Data protection and user privacy rights | Partially Addressed |
| GDPR | Data deletion and user control | Addressed |
| GDPR | Transparency about data processing | Addressed |
| CCPA/CPRA | Privacy disclosures | Addressed |
| CCPA/CPRA | User control over personal information | Partially Addressed |
| Data Security | Protection of personal information | Addressed |
| International Transfers | Transfer of data across countries | Requires Attention |

## Key Compliance Requirements

A privacy-compliant organization should:

1. Collect personal data for legitimate and clearly explained purposes.
2. Collect only the data that is necessary.
3. Clearly inform users about how their data is processed.
4. Provide appropriate mechanisms for accessing or deleting personal information.
5. Protect personal information using suitable security measures.
6. Properly manage third-party data processors.
7. Follow applicable requirements for international data transfers.

## Observations

LeetCode provides a privacy policy explaining its collection and use of personal information. It also provides mechanisms for account deletion and describes security measures.

However, compliance requirements can vary depending on the user's location, applicable law, and specific processing activity. Therefore, international data transfers, third-party processing, data retention, and user privacy rights should be reviewed regularly.

## Recommendations

- Regularly review compliance with applicable privacy regulations.
- Keep privacy notices clear and up to date.
- Provide transparent information about data collection and processing.
- Maintain proper records of data processing activities.
- Review third-party service providers for privacy compliance.
- Strengthen mechanisms for user data access and deletion.
- Regularly review international data-transfer practices.

## Conclusion

The assessment shows that LeetCode has several privacy practices that support regulatory compliance, including privacy disclosures, security measures, and account deletion mechanisms. However, continuous monitoring is required because privacy regulations and organizational data-processing practices can change over time.

### Overall Compliance Status: Partially Compliant / Requires Continuous Review
