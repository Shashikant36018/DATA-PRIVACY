# Practical 1: Data Privacy Audit of LeetCode

## Aim

To audit the data privacy practices of LeetCode and identify potential privacy risks and improvements.

## Organization

**LeetCode** is an online programming and educational platform where users solve coding problems, participate in contests, and learn programming.

## Data Identified

LeetCode may collect information such as:

- Name and email
- IP address
- Cookies
- Account information
- Usage and log information
- User-submitted content

## Audit Areas

The following areas were examined:

| Area | Observation | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Data Collection | User and usage information is collected | Medium |
| Cookies | Cookies are used for website functionality and analytics | Medium |
| Data Sharing | Information may be shared with service providers | Medium |
| Security | Security measures are described in the privacy policy | Low-Medium |
| International Transfer | Data may be processed in other countries | Medium |
| Account Deletion | Users can request account deletion | Low |
| User Content | Users can submit programming-related content | Medium |

## Major Risks

1. Third-party data processing
2. International data transfers
3. Cookie and usage tracking
4. User-submitted content

## Recommendations

- Collect only necessary information.
- Clearly explain data collection and cookie usage.
- Limit third-party access to personal information.
- Apply appropriate encryption and access controls.
- Provide clear information about data deletion and retention.
- Regularly review privacy practices.

## Conclusion

The audit shows that LeetCode has several privacy controls and provides a privacy policy and account deletion mechanism. However, areas such as third-party processing, cookies, international data transfers, and user content require continued privacy attention.

### Overall Risk: Medium
